TEEMING / MUSIC WORKBENCH / PASS 02

Space. Material. Something underneath.

Nine longer studies and six short cues, written from scratch. More held resonance and empty space; no sampled strings or lead-and-accompaniment score. These are directions to choose between, not a finished soundtrack.

The original three drafts are preserved here. These previews haven't replaced the music in the game.

01 / FOREST / BREATH / 1:36

Root Lung

Long, low breaths caught inside a wooden body. A few hollow knocks; the middle empties out.

Warmest option. Is the quiet horn-like body useful when it never becomes a tune?

After the Last Shift

Wide low drum skins, steel cavities, a tired pipe breath and an occasional uneven bell. Rhythms stop before settling into a loop.

07 / THREAT / BOSS STUDY / 1:12

Do Not Wake the Press

A weight-bearing pulse with missing steps. Skin and struck steel gather, then leave a hole where the next hit should be.

A more urgent option without a heroic tune; should combat music go this far?
