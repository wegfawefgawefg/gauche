Proposed replacement for the paused full-game goal, 2026-09-16. Task status remains in [MASTER_TASKS.md](../MASTER_TASKS.md); original messages remain in [user notes](../user_messages/2026-09-16-generation-feedback.txt). Baseline tag: `forest-gen-baseline-2026-09-16` (`9d1559e`).

## Objective to activate

Focus exclusively on Gauche's ordinary Forest floors 1-1 through 1-4 and a usable generation/playtest inspector. First deliver a controller-accessible main-menu Dev → World Gen view with floor switching, repeated regeneration, visible/copyable seed, pan/zoom/fit, overlay/lighting toggles and generation annotations. A Play button must enter the exact inspected floor without rerolling its terrain, entities or loot; returning to inspection must support replaying that original generated state. Show the generator revision and relevant settings with the seed.

Then iterate the Forest generator and its integrated content into a rich, varied, interactive world: nested weighted components, context-dependent population and loot, cross-room carving/growth, open connected sectors, sprawling roots and spider habitats, whole-level rivers, and compatible major/minor themes. Rework the existing giant tree, timber grove and snake pit to produce different tactical situations within the same landmark identity. Develop ant colonies/hauling crews, mushroom forests and reactive gnome settlements, including ant riders, as interacting systems in ordinary generation. Tune four distinct stage profiles: lively/simple 1-1, escalating combinations in 1-2, full Forest complexity in 1-3, boss and Ice-border transition in 1-4. Optional rewards need not each supply their solution beside them.

Use the pinned baseline for comparison. Follow the live checklist entries for this scope, preserve required progression, deterministic co-op and reasonable performance, and use code-generated art. Commit/push cohesive milestones. Verify through builds, bounded generation audits and focused render/system checks; no autonomous playthroughs or large new test suite. The user owns playtesting. Deliver the inspector early and continue incorporating their seed-specific feedback; initial feature presence alone does not establish replay variety or acceptance. Leave unrelated biome/music/catalog work deferred. Do not declare the Forest iteration complete until the scoped systems are integrated and the user accepts its variety and playtesting workflow.
