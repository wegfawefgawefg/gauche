# Working title: Teeming

2026-09-17. User approved Teeming for the public game title and browser release.
An initial Steam store/API and web search found no video game titled exactly
Teeming; The Teeming Shadowfell is a D&D supplement on itch.io. This was a
storefront check, not trademark clearance. No domain has been purchased.

The user feels the game has found its identity: crowded, lively worlds with
creatures doing things independently of the player, and difficult survival among
them. Forest's current balance is approved as a reference, not a retuning request.
See the [measured Forest baseline](../measurements/forest-2026-09-17/README.md).

## User's trailer sketch

> teeming is a game... about life  
> show different panning scenes of ants getting food, gnomes, chickens
>
> perhaps too much life.
>
> i... am going to lose my life

Possible edit: linger on the ants carrying food, gnomes going home and chickens
wandering; let their ambient sounds carry the opening. After “perhaps too much
life,” pull back to reveal the player being overwhelmed. Deliver the last line
over actual chaotic play. Keep the pause and the understated delivery.

## Name shortlist

| Name | What it suggests / tradeoff |
|---|---|
| **Teeming** | Preferred candidate. Abundant life, movement and crowding; works beyond Forest. |
| **Too Much Life** | Strong connection to the trailer joke; longer, more overtly comic. |
| **Seething** | More threatening and hostile; loses some affection for the creatures. |
| **Brimming** | Abundance and fullness; softer, less dangerous. |
| **Wild Company** | Creatures and multiplayer companions; less immediate about density. |
| **Life Everywhere** | Clear ecological identity; less compact. |
| **Alive and Kicking** | Life plus fighting; a familiar phrase, less distinctive. |
| **Overrun** | Communicates danger and crowds; implies a more exclusively combat-focused game. |

Domain ideas for a later availability check: `teeming.com`, `teeminggame.com`,
`playteeming.com`. A project subdomain can serve a browser prototype before choosing
or purchasing a domain. Keep repository/binary/package names unchanged until the
title is settled.
