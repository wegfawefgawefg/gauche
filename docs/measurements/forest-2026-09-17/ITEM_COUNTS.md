# Forest item probabilities and quantities

Build ec3b1ec. 256 independent ordinary maps for each floor (1,024 total). Each cell is **chance of at least one / average individual item units per floor**, including zero-item floors. A stack of three bandages contributes three units, not one. Equipment counts are actual copies; herb bags count bags, not charges; ammo counts refill items, not bullets. Forest totals sum the four per-floor sample means, not measured player inventories or simulated whole-run outcomes.

Ground pickups across the entire map, including optional or hard-to-reach locations and fish in water. Excludes initial player gear, carried-over equipment, inter-floor offers/shops, later random drops, hidden wall contents and stored entity loot. Those latter two sources are appended separately.
